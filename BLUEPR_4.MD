*Phase 2 blueprint · vrajvithalani.com · Tranche 4 of 7 · Covers 12 city × service location pages (Surat, Ahmedabad, Bangalore × Google Ads, SEO, Web Development, CRO & Automation). Word count: 1,500–2,000 each. Not spun copies of service pages — each page carries genuine local landmarks, local relevance, local FAQ, and LocalBusiness schema with geo coordinates.*

*Anchoring pattern (locked in Tranche 1's Location-Page Anchoring Decision):*
- *Surat pages: full LocalBusiness schema with Surat address + geo coordinates.*

*Reference structural template: `05_example_blueprint_reference.md` Page 3 (Cosmetic Dentist in Satellite). Every location page follows that pattern — hero + short-answer + services offered here + why locals choose Vraj + reaching us / working with us + local reviews + hours + local FAQ + nearby areas.*

*Site-Wide Standards, Person schema, and LocalBusiness Reference Block all live in Tranche 1 and apply here.*
