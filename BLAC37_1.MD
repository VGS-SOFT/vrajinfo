Every case study follows the same template — client context → challenge → approach → what I did → results → tech / tools → lessons — but leans into the specific proof each project offers.*

*Featured (live or launching soon): Powercable, Drvishva, Vitthalshringar, Trust NGO — 2,000–2,500 words each with linked live URLs and full testimonial assets.*

*Older nameable (from VGS IT Solution era, most domains now expired): Dreams Astro Numero, VM Graphite, Little Genius, Parv Travels, Synergy Tutorials, Soni Classes, VGS IT Solution, VGS Store, IOTA ANPR — 1,500–2,000 words each, historic framing.*

*Locked constraints (from user brief and Doc 02):*
- *Single-actor framing throughout — every action Vraj's own; no partners, no team, no dissolution drama.*
- *No named individuals other than Vraj anywhere.*
- *IOTA ANPR: overview-level only. No implementation details.*
- *VGS IT Solution: framed as "founded, ran for 18 months, transitioned by choice."*
- *Little Genius: include the 10x brand awareness lift (500–600 → 5,500–6,000 monthly views).*
- *Powercable: ~40-day cold-call-to-deployment timeline is story-worthy.*
- *Silent Indian SMB lens throughout — never labeled.*
- *Testimonial attribution: company-only (no named individuals within client companies).*

*Site-Wide Standards and reference blocks live in Tranche 1.*

## Case-study template (shared structure, all 13 pages)

Every case study follows this H2 sequence (with client-specific H3s inside "What I actually did"):

- H1
- Author byline strip (name + role + published date + last-updated)
- **Short-answer block** (60–80 words — third person, entity-loaded: client name + vertical + primary service delivered + headline result)
- H2 — The client (company context, vertical, scale)
- H2 — The challenge (what the client came to Vraj with, or what Vraj diagnosed on arrival)
- H2 — My approach (framework / philosophy applied to this specific problem)
- H2 — What I actually did (2–5 H3 subsections per client, describing the deliverables in first person)
- H2 — Results (metrics where available, honest qualitative framing where not)
- H2 — Tech and tools used (specific tool naming — builder credibility)
- H2 — What I learned / lessons for similar businesses (transferable insight)
- H2 — Want similar results for your [service] project? (soft CTA to relevant service pillar — NOT to `/contact/` per critical rule 9)
- Author bio card
