# Phase 1b — Keyword Research within Territory

*Per-topic keyword targeting, live SERP analysis, and revised priority tiers for the 126 candidate topics from Phase 1a. This document is the direct input for Phase 2 (blueprint) — each entry gives the blueprint author their primary keyword, variants, intent, competitive read, and differentiation angle.*

---

## Methodology and honest limits

**What was done:** ~35 targeted Google searches during July 2026 covering the representative query in each sub-territory, then extrapolated SERP patterns across similar-shaped topics inside that cluster. Verified: what type of content ranks, whether AI Overviews appear, how entrenched the top-ranking domains are, whether the SERP is dominated by ad-driven affiliates / generic agency blogs / official docs / user-generated content.

**What was NOT done:** No paid-tool volume or difficulty numbers (Ahrefs / SEMrush / Ubersuggest / GKP). Where a search-volume decision matters (e.g., "is this worth writing at all?"), the entry is marked `[VOLUME CHECK]` for you to spot-verify in Google Trends or Keyword Planner during Phase 2 or before writing.

**Difficulty scale (SERP-inferred, not tool-derived):**
- **Low** — SERP has thin content, forum answers, or no clear authority; a well-written piece can rank fast.
- **Medium** — Mixed SERP with some authoritative domains but obvious gaps for a differentiated angle.
- **Med-High** — 2–4 entrenched domains (WordStream, HubSpot, Neil Patel, Google's own docs) sit in top slots but topical specificity or freshness can break in.
- **High** — SERP is a wall of entrenched authorities with recent updates; ranking requires either superior depth, uniquely defensible angle, or targeting long-tail variants.

**Tier action codes:**
- `T1 ✓` / `T2 ✓` / `T3 ✓` — original Phase 1a tier confirmed by SERP evidence.
- `→ T1` / `→ T2` / `→ T3` — promoted or demoted based on SERP evidence.
- `[VERIFY]` — NGO topic pending Trust-project depth confirmation before writing.
- `[VOLUME CHECK]` — worth a 30-second Keyword Planner check before committing writing time.
- `CUT` — SERP evidence says this topic is not worth the write.

**Silent-angle reminder:** every "Angle" note leans on the Indian SMB reality lens without naming it as a framework. This is how the differentiation actually shows up in the final article, not how we brand it.

# Cross-cutting SERP observations for Phase 2

1. **AI Overviews now appear on ~40%+ of queries** across the topic set. Every Tier 1 topic should be written with a strong short-answer block near the top (first 100 words) plus a well-structured FAQ near the bottom. This is the biggest single tactical shift since Phase 1a was drafted.

2. **The Indian-INR framing is a genuine competitive moat.** Every SERP that included budget/cost content was dominated by USD-denominated US-audience content. Any topic where Vraj can anchor an example in real Indian rupees has an under-competed angle available — this validates the silent Indian SMB lens strongly.

3. **The compound (cross-zone) topics have the thinnest SERPs.** Topics that require both "runs the ads" and "builds the pages" or both "runs the marketing" and "codes in Python" have almost no honest competition. This is where Vraj's differentiation is genuinely defensible — and it argues for promoting more cross-zone topics into T1 over time.

4. **GEO topics are early-market.** Search Engine Journal / Neil Patel / HubSpot have not yet fully claimed this territory. Move fast on GEO topics (T45–T49) — this window will close in 12–18 months.

5. **Agency lead-magnet content dominates several corners.** Google Ads hiring (T97), coaching institute marketing (T81), local citation building (T42) — these SERPs are cluttered with agency-selling-you content. Vraj's honest, non-selling voice differentiates here, but rankings will be harder and slower than the SERP-thin corners.

6. **Overlaps to resolve in Phase 2:**
   - T51 (fast WP without caching-plugin fight) and T63 (WP speed on shared hosting) — either merge or clearly split on plugins vs server-side.
   - T66 (Shopify vs WooCommerce India D2C) and T87 (Shopify vs WooCommerce first-time founder) — merge into one long-form, or clearly split on platform-first vs founder-first framings.

7. **Candidate additions the SERP surfaced (flagged for approval, not added):**
   - "AI Overviews for local service businesses" (specific slice with real 2026 relevance)
