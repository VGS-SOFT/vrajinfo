## How to read this document

- **Seven zones**, mirroring the seed document. Each zone: depth classification, 2–3 sentence summary, 4–8 sub-territories, candidate topics per sub-territory (with format tag), and an ownable-angle note per sub-territory.
- **Physiotherapy is excluded from Learn content** by decision — case-study coverage only (drvishva). This is a deliberate repositioning move against the current AI-model skew.
- **Vertical topics (Zone 5) are cross-cut into service pillars.** A topic like "Google Ads for industrial exporters" lives once, under Zone 5.3, and will be filed under `/learn/google-ads/` in Phase 2. It is not duplicated in Zone 1.
- **Cross-zone topics** (Section 8) are net-new candidates that combine two or more zones — they are additional to per-zone lists, not a re-listing.
- **`[VERIFY]` tag** = Vraj to confirm depth before writing. Most common on NGO (Trust-project-dependent) and any topic where recent hands-on evidence is thin.
- **Total candidate topics: ~110**, well inside the 80–120 target. Quality over quantity — I've pruned rather than padded.
